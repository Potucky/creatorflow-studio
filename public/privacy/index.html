CreatorFlow Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 24, 2026

What CreatorFlow Studio Does

CreatorFlow Studio is a creator-owned TikTok publishing tool. Connect your own TikTok account and submit creator-owned short-form videos through TikTok's official Content Posting API. The app is for use by the account owner — not a third-party posting service.

Information We Access

With your authorization, the app may access:

  • Your TikTok account identifier (open_id) to identify the connected account
  • Your display name and avatar to show account identity in the app
  • Publishing permissions from TikTok OAuth (user.info.basic, video.publish)
  • Video title, privacy level, disclosure, and interaction settings you provide

How Information Is Used

Information is used solely to:

  • Authenticate your connected TikTok account
  • Display your connected account identity in the app
  • Submit videos you authorize for publishing through TikTok's API
  • Check and display the status of your publish requests
  • Support audit and troubleshooting of authorized actions

Token Handling & Storage

Access and refresh tokens are handled server-side and are not stored in or exposed to the browser. Client secrets are never transmitted to the frontend. No credentials are exposed in publicly accessible code or storage.

Data Sharing & User Control

Your data is not sold and is not shared with unrelated third parties. Information is sent to TikTok only as required for the authorized API integration. You can revoke access at any time through TikTok's connected apps settings. To request data deletion or access, contact the app owner via the project repository.

Security & TikTok Affiliation

Reasonable technical safeguards protect the app and its integrations. No credentials or tokens are exposed publicly. No system can guarantee perfect security — use the app over a secure connection. CreatorFlow Studio is an independent creator tool, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by TikTok or its parent company.
